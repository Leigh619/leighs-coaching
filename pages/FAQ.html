1. What results can I expect from coaching?

Most clients see improvements in strength, physique, and energy within the first 4–6 weeks. Physical changes vary by person, but the focus is on sustainable progress, not quick fixes.

2. Who is this for?

Online coaching is for people who want a clear plan, personalised guidance, and accountability rather than trying to figure everything out themselves. It's particularly suited to people who want to:

✅Lose body fat without following restrictive diets or constantly starting over.

✅Build muscle and improve their physique with a structured training programme.

✅Get stronger and more confident in the gym or training at home.

✅Improve their nutrition without being told they need to eat "perfectly."

✅Stay consistent with regular check-ins, accountability and ongoing support.

✅Train around a busy lifestyle, with a programme built around your schedule, equipment and experience.

✅Have someone to guide them through the process, answer questions and make adjustments when things aren't working.

3. Who it isn't for:

Coaching isn't designed for people looking for a quick fix, extreme diets, or a generic workout plan. If you're willing to put in consistent effort and want a sustainable approach to training, nutrition, and lifestyle change, coaching could be a great fit.

4. Do I need gym experience to start?

No. Many clients begin as complete beginners. Your programme is tailored to your level, equipment, and confidence — whether that’s a full gym or a few dumbbells at home.

5. How does online coaching work?

Online coaching works exactly the same no matter where you live. You’ll get a personalised training plan, nutrition guidance, weekly check-ins, form feedback, and ongoing support through WhatsApp or Discord. Everything is delivered remotely, so you can follow your programme from anywhere in the world with clear structure, accountability, and guidance.

7. Do I need to track calories?

Not unless you want to. If you opt for a personalised meal plan you will not need to track calorie intake.

8. How long do I need to commit?

Most clients choose to work with me for 5 months or more to build momentum and consistency, but you can continue month-to-month. Online coaching is flexible and works no matter where you live.

9. What if I have injuries or limitations?

Your programme is adapted around any injuries, mobility issues, or confidence barriers. Everything is built to be safe, progressive, and achievable — even when training remotely.

10. How do payments work?

Payments are taken monthly via stripe, paypal or bank transfer. There are no hidden fees, and you can cancel anytime with notice. Online coaching is available worldwide, so payments work the same no matter where you live.

11. Will you help with nutrition as well as training?

Yes — you get evidence-based nutrition guidance tailored to your goals, preferences, and lifestyle. Everything is delivered online, so you can follow your plan from anywhere in the world without restrictive diets or “clean eating” rules.

12. Can I work with you if I'm based locally?

Yes. Although most of my coaching is delivered online, I also work with clients locally who prefer in-person support. If you're based nearby, get in touch and we can discuss which option would suit you best.
